Android (Chrome)

  1. Open the link in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install at the top of the app, or tap the ⋮ menu → Add to Home screen / Install app.
  3. Open Green Book from your home screen.

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Open the link in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open Green Book from your home screen.

First time you open it

  1. The app asks for your PDF copy of the Green Book. Tap Choose the Green Book PDF and pick the file (it's often in Downloads).
  2. That's it. The book stays on your phone, so you only do this once, and the app works without internet after that.

The hymn book PDF is not included in the app. Each person needs their own copy on their phone.
